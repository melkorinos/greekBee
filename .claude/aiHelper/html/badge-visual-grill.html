2. What the refinement changed review this

One change was substantive rather than cosmetic, and you can reject it.

Γραμμές — before 3 sleepers

Near-parallel rails with three evenly spaced crossbars. This is the version that reads as a ladder at chip size.

Γραμμές — after 2 sleepers

Two crossbars instead of three, and a much wider taper: the rails now converge from 21 units apart at the base to 3 at the top. Perspective is what separates a track from a ladder, so the taper does the work the crossbars were failing to do.

The other four

Cosmetic only. Κορυφή narrowed and re-centred; κεραυνός widened and its waist thickened, because it was the lightest mark in the set; λίθος trimmed slightly, because it was the heaviest; πέταλα thickened from 3.1 to 3.2 semi-minor so the ring of six survives 12px.
