The mark — decided

Card 18 and icon 1, chosen 16 August 2026 and shipped the same day. This page is now the record of that decision rather than a question: the alternatives are kept so the choice can be re-read in context, not re-opened. The chosen two carry a yellow flag. Nothing here is waiting on a reply.

What the pick became. One drawing, src/app/_brand/fan.tsx, rendered at three sizes: opengraph-image.tsx (1200×630 share card), icon.tsx (32 px tab) and apple-icon.tsx (180 px home screen). One module rather than three drawings is the whole reason a favicon cannot drift away from the share card. The stock Create-Next-App favicon.ico, which served the Next.js logo in production until this landed, is deleted.
Two things the real render showed that these previews could not. First, the card came out regular weight, not bold — every candidate here is drawn bold, but the face ImageResponse bundles has only one weight. Fixed: the mark now ships a 12 KB subset of Inter Bold, so what deploys matches what you approved. Second, at 16 px icon 1's three letters really are noise. That was the known cost of picking the highest-fidelity icon, and icon 2 — the same fan without the flanking letters — stays a one-line change if the tab icon ever wants to be cleaner.
The font is 12 KB, not 350. The old warning on this page priced a full Greek face. The mark needs the Latin alphabet plus six Greek glyphs, and the Google Fonts text= parameter cuts a font to exactly that. A character missing from a subset renders as nothing rather than erroring, so a test reads the font's own glyph table and fails if the wordmark or a tile letter ever moves outside what it carries.

Icons — 180 px home-screen tile, 64, 32 px tab, 16 px dense tab

1. The full fan CHOSEN — SHIPPED

Chosen, and shipped as src/app/icon.tsx (32 px) and apple-icon.tsx (180 px). The card shrunk to a square, letters and all — best fidelity to the card, and the one that gives up most at 16 px. That trade was made knowingly; the rendered 32 px is a shade muddier still than this scaled preview.

2. The fan, no letters

Same shape, letters dropped. The fan is the recognisable part, not the glyphs — this keeps the part that survives and throws away the part that turns to mush.

3. The centre tile, framed

The yellow tile lifted straight out of the fan and set square. Reads at every size, and the card still explains where it came from.

4. The centre tile, full bleed

No frame — the tile is the icon. The loudest of the eight in a tab strip, and the furthest from card 18's dark ground.

5. Two tiles

The fan cut to two. Keeps the overlap and the tilt — the two things that say more-than-one-game — while giving Λ room to stay legible.

6. Peek

One legible letter, with green and teal showing only as slivers behind it. All three colours survive to 16 px because none of them has to carry a glyph.

7. The tight fan

Middle ground: three tiles, barely tilted, and only the centre one lettered. Calmer than 1 and still unmistakably the card's shape.

8. The fan on white

Icon 1 on a light ground, to pair with card 18b. Worth a look on a dark browser theme, where a white tile is the one that stands out.

Lockups — the same mark at 44 px beside the wordmark, light bar and dark

A favicon is the mark alone. A lockup is that mark next to the word — the shape a header or a footer needs. Nothing ships a lockup yet: no header or footer uses one today, and this section exists so that when one is wanted it is drawn from the mark already chosen rather than invented again.

That cannot happen before a deploy, and it cannot happen on a Vercel preview either: previews on this project are SSO-protected and answer 302 to anyone unauthenticated, and Facebook's and Viber's scrapers carry no session.
