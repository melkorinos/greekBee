Share card + icon candidates — TICKET-10

Twenty Open Graph cards, each drawn at its true 1200×630 and scaled to half here, plus ten icon candidates at the sizes that actually ship. Every colour is one the site already uses: the platform yellow, and the ten per-game accents. Reply with one card number and one icon number — for example “card 11, icon 4”.

Why almost none of them have Greek text in the image. The card is generated by Next's ImageResponse, which ships exactly one font — Geist — and Geist covers six Greek codepoints: Λ Ω λ μ π ω. Everything else, every accent and the final ς, comes out a blank box. A Greek line inside the image means committing a real font file to the repo (~350 KB). Cards marked needs a font pay that; the rest are free. Either way the Greek line still reaches the reader — Messenger, Viber and Facebook print og:description as text under the image, so Greek inside the image is a second copy, not the only one.

Λ is used as the mark because it is the platform's own initial and one of the six free glyphs. Icons 8 and 10 use no letter at all, so they escape the constraint entirely.
