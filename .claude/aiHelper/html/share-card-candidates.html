Share card + icon candidates — TICKET-10

Twenty-five Open Graph cards, each drawn at its true 1200×630 and scaled to half, plus twelve icons at the sizes that actually ship. Every one of them says word game through its shape — a guess board, a crossword, a rack of letter tiles — before any text is read. Palette is dark grey, green, teal and yellow, nothing else. Reply with one card number and one icon number.

The one technical constraint. The card is generated by Next's ImageResponse, which ships a single font that covers six Greek letters — Λ Ω λ μ π ω — and nothing else: no accents, no final ς. Cards built from those six, from Latin letters, or from digits are free. Cards with real Greek words are marked needs a font and cost one font file committed to the repo (~350 KB). Everything else about them is identical, so the mark is about cost, not quality.

Whatever you pick, I rebuild it in the generator — these are drawn in the same flexbox subset the generator understands, so what you see is what deploys.
