launch is an act of promotion, not a change in exposure; that framing is what made the checklist
finite. **`launch-readiness.md` question 1 is RESOLVED** and holds the blocking/accepted split, the
five tickets it produced (`TICKET-06`–`10`) and the release-day runbook. **Only sequencing is open.**

  This retires the Leksindeseis-pool, wip-flip and content-supply threads entirely. `TICKET-06`
  introduces a **third registry state**: `wip` = unfinished, `hidden` = deliberately not shown; ADR
  owed, because the `games.ts` header documents its state design and a future session will otherwise
  collapse the two flags back into one.
- **Runbook order is load-bearing**: deploy → **verify prod serves the merge commit** → `db:backup`
  off-machine → `launch-reset.sql` → announce. The reset must follow the deploy (`BadgeMark` is on
  `dev` only, so running it early re-earns every badge against the retired emoji glyphs), and the
  dump is the only undo that exists on a Free-plan project with no PITR.
- **Accepted as-is:** terms of service (not written), E2E depth (gate = existing suites green,
  `ISSUE-03`), backups automation (`ISSUE-01`), API rate limiting, Λογοπαίγνιο's trademark note.

| **Leksokipos difficulty** | **s131 rebalance — do not re-litigate the direction.** `softCap` is the ENTIRE source of small-vs-big rank unfairness, not a contributor: without it `frac_of_garden = SCORE_SCALE × top%` for every puzzle identically (`mean × n ≡ total`), spread 0.009. It discounts only the ~75% of puzzles above the knee, so rich gardens were the *fair* ones being made cheap — «make big puzzles harder» is the correct goal, «small puzzles are too hard» is the symptom. The cap STAYS (removing it: words p90 68→214, max 728; trimming to compensate keeps <56% of the corpus) — it deliberately trades fairness for a tedium ceiling. **`SCORE_SCALE` is the only knob that reaches sub-knee puzzles** — tuning knee/k/top% cannot help them, ever. Shipped: `SCORE_SCALE` **0.75**, `SOFT_CAP_K` **400**, knee 400, rank threshold **80% unchanged**. **Never move the top-rank threshold to "fix" difficulty** — `leksokipos-stin-korifi` fires on `rank === TOP_RANK` and achievement ids FREEZE on first deploy; `achievementTuning.theristisFoundRatio: 0.8` is an unrelated 80%, never sync them. Quality gates (`MAX_PANGRAMS: 20`, `MAX_WORDS_TO_GENIUS: 107`) live in gameRules, enforced by `scripts/lib/leksokipos/puzzleQuality.ts` (imports the real `scoreWord`/`softCap`/`isPangram` — a reimplementation here is the top drift risk; parity-tested against `maxScore`). Both gates earn their keep (19 boards failed pangram-only). `realisticWordsToGenius` = a RELATIVE index (genius ÷ mean word score), optimistic — never player-facing copy. Corpus pruned 1008→**799**, dates reflowed to 2028-05-31. Generators enforce the gates (batch rejects + releases the date; generate-puzzle warns). **Date reflow invariant:** `getPuzzleForDate`/`getPuzzleStubForDate` fall back to the LAST puzzle on a miss, so a gap silently serves the 2028 board instead of throwing — `puzzleCorpusQuality.test.ts` date-integrity block is mandatory.
